# Enso Product FAQ
Classification: Internal

## How do I reset a client's password?
Direct the client to the self-service reset link on the login page. Support staff should never
ask for or handle a client's password directly.

## What are the supported browsers?
The current and prior major versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari are supported.

## How do I export a session report?
Open the session, choose Reports, then Export. Reports are generated as PDF or CSV.

## Where can clients find billing history?
Under Account > Billing. Only account owners can see full billing history.
