# Enso Support Triage
Classification: Internal

## Severity levels
- P1: production down for many customers. Page on-call immediately.
- P2: major feature broken, no workaround. Respond within 1 hour.
- P3: minor issue with a workaround. Respond within 1 business day.

## Escalation path
Tier 1 -> Tier 2 -> Engineering on-call. Always attach the session id and a reproduction.

## Common triage questions
Confirm the affected account, the browser, and whether the issue is reproducible before escalating.
