# Enso Benefits FAQ 2026
Classification: Confidential
Department: HR

This document is for HR and cleared staff only. It is intentionally classified higher than the
support FAQs so the lab can show a support user (max clearance: Internal) being denied access at the
retrieval layer.

## Open enrollment
Open enrollment runs in November. Elections made during enrollment take effect on January 1.

## Medical plan tiers
Three tiers are offered. Premiums vary by tier and coverage level; see the HR portal for the
current rate sheet.

## Parental leave
Eligible employees receive paid parental leave per the current policy. Contact HR for specifics.
