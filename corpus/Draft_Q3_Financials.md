## Why this is Restricted

These are **unreleased** results and forward guidance. Premature disclosure is **material non-public
information (MNPI)** — trading on it, or tipping someone who trades, carries securities-law
(insider-trading) exposure. The sensitivity here is **market/legal**, distinct from the trade-secret
sensitivity of the ranking design and the privacy sensitivity of the employee roster. Same
classification level (Restricted), three different reasons.
